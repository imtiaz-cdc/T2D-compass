00:00 - A clear starting point
This is T two D Compass. It helps researchers explore completed type two diabetes studies and keep the source close to every summary. The idea is simple: find a study, inspect what the registry actually says, and carry that evidence into review. Let's walk through the app, then look at how we checked it.

00:24 - Why this workflow matters
A study's title rarely tells the whole story. We still need its design, enrollment, interventions, outcomes, and results status. We also need to know what is missing. Collecting those details repeatedly is the task Compass supports. It organizes public registry information into a consistent card, while keeping the original record available for the questions a summary cannot answer.

00:52 - Two tools, one source
There are two tools behind the workflow. One finds candidate completed studies. The other retrieves a card for a specific trial identifier. Both read public ClinicalTrials dot gov records. The browser and the optional assistant use these same tools. Let's start with a search.

Compass supports research preparation. It does not rank treatments or recommend care.
