00:20 - A repeatable research task
PURPOSE
Researchers repeatedly collect study identifiers, design, enrollment, interventions, registered outcomes, and results availability. Compass organizes the registry fields into a consistent evidence card, keeping source links and review flags attached. Its intended output is a starting point for human review. It does not turn registry descriptions into a treatment recommendation.
Evidence: README.md | Research workflow and interpretation limits

00:38 - Two tools with clear purposes
IMPLEMENTED SCOPE
The first tool searches for completed interventional studies with an explicit supported type two diabetes condition label. The second retrieves an evidence card for a specified trial identifier. Both tools are read only and use a fixed public source, with no patient records. The browser and optional Databricks assistant use these same tools.
